  2. Supporting the care of cows

  3. Promoting sustainable practices

    Organic farming and tree plantation drives.

  4. Gaining divine blessings

  6. Being part of a noble cause

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I donate?

You can donate through the online option on this page once it is live, by bank transfer or UPI (see the Transparency page), or in person at the ashram.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. Send your payment screenshot on WhatsApp for an official receipt. Monetary donations are eligible for an 80G certificate.

What are the 80G benefits?

Donations are tax exempted under section 80G(5)(iii) of the Income Tax Act, registration No AABTH0927L22LK01.

How are the funds used?

Funds go towards the care of cows, the education and upkeep of Gurukul children, and environment work.

Can I volunteer or donate in kind?

Please contact us on WhatsApp and we will guide you.
