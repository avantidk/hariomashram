Gurukul

We are reviving the spirit of the gurukul: learning rooted in Vedic tradition and blended with modern education, for children who need it most.

Support for needy children

Our gurukul shelters around 30 underprivileged children and looks after them as one family.

What every child receives

Education

Children attend nearby day schools.

Nutritious meals

Healthy meals every day.

Clothing & necessities

Medical care

Regular check-ups and treatment.

Shelter

A safe and caring home.

Kanya Gurukul, Mundakheda

In collaboration with Kanya Gurukul, Mundakheda (Laksar, Uttarakhand), which shelters and educates over 50 underprivileged girls under the leadership of Dr. Savita.
