Animal Rescue

A dedicated team of young volunteers, our Gaurakshaks, rescues animals in need and brings them to safety.

When we step in

  • Cattle injured on the roadside after accidents
  • Animals abandoned by farmers facing financial difficulty
  • Animals rescued from illegal traffickers and butchers
  • Animals kept in poor conditions in dairies
  • Other emergencies

Why rescuing large cattle is hard

Fear and aggression

Frightened animals can react aggressively.

Risk of injury

Handlers can get hurt during the rescue.

Heavy weight

Large animals need many hands to move.

Limited space

Tight spots where lifting machinery cannot reach.

Manual effort

Much of the work is done by hand.

Transport to the shelter

Moving injured animals safely over distance.

Rescue ambulance

Our specialised ambulance has an automatic hydraulic lift that helps us move large injured animals safely.

After the rescue

  1. Taken to the nearest gaushala

    The animal is moved to safety without delay.

  2. Primary treatment

    A doctor examines and treats the animal.

  3. Food, water and medication

    Care continues until the animal recovers.

Care our rescued cows receive

Nutritious fodder

Clean water
