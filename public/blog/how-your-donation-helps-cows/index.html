How your donation helps the cows

21 November 2026

Most of the support we receive goes into two basic needs: fodder and medical care. A fodder kit helps feed the cows for a day, and a medical aid kit helps treat animals that arrive injured or unwell.

Every contribution, large or small, helps. You can donate from our donation page, and donations are eligible for tax exemption under section 80G.
