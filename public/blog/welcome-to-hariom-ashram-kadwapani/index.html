Welcome to Hariom Ashram Kadwapani

22 November 2026

Hariom Ashram Kadwapani is a community-run ashram at Kadwapani, Karbari Grant, Dehradun. Our work rests on three pillars: caring for cows in our gaushala, educating children in our gurukul, and protecting the environment.

This blog will share updates from the ashram, stories of rescued animals, news from the gurukul and ways you can take part. Follow us on social media or message us on WhatsApp to stay in touch.
