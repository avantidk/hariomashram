About Us

Hariom Ashram Kadwapani is a community-run ashram at Kadwapani, Karbari Grant, Dehradun. We shelter and treat cows, run a gurukul for children, and organise tree plantation and awareness drives with local villagers.

Our work rests on one belief: service to living beings, education and care for nature belong together.
