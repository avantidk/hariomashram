About Us

Hariom Ashram Kadwapani was founded on the belief that service to living beings, education and care for the environment belong together.

What We Do

Three pillars guide everything the ashram does.

Gaushala & Animal Rescue

Shelter, fodder and medical care for rescued, abandoned and injured cows.

Gurukul

Value-based education for children, combining studies, Sanskrit and life skills.
🌳

Environment

Tree plantation, organic farming and awareness drives for a greener Uttarakhand.
