Gaushala & Animal Rescue

Our gaushala gives shelter, fodder and medical care to cows that are rescued, abandoned or injured. The cows live in open, shaded sheds with space to move and fresh fodder every day.

  • Rescue of abandoned and injured cows
  • Daily fodder and clean water
  • Medical treatment and care for calves
