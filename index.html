About Us

Hariom Ashram Kadwapani was founded on the belief that service to living beings, education and care for the environment belong together.

Read more

What We Do

Three pillars guide everything the ashram does.

Animal Rescue

Rescue, treatment and care for injured and abandoned animals.

Gaushalas

Three gaushalas giving shelter, fodder and medical care to cows.

Gurukul

Education, meals, shelter and care for underprivileged children.

Environment

Tree plantation, organic farming and river cleanliness drives.
