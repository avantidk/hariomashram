About Hariom Ashram

Service to living beings, education and nature, together

Founded in 2006, Hariom Ashram is a not-for-profit organisation at Kadwapani, Dehradun. Every rupee donated goes towards building well-run shelters for stray cows and caring for children.

  • Registered with the Animal Welfare Board
  • 80G tax-exempt donations
  • Bank details published openly

What we do

Three pillars of our work

Animal Rescue

Rescue, treatment and care for injured and abandoned animals.

Gaushalas

Three gaushalas giving shelter, fodder and medical care to cows.

Gurukul

Education, meals, shelter and care for underprivileged children.

Environment

Tree plantation, organic farming and river cleanliness drives.
