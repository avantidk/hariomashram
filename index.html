About Us

Hariom Ashram Kadwapani was founded on the belief that service to living beings, education, and care for the environment belong together.

Based at Kadwapani, Karbari Grant, Dehradun, we shelter and treat cows, run a gurukul for children, and organise tree plantation and cleanliness drives with local villagers.

What We Do

Three pillars guide everything the ashram does.

🐄

Gaushala & Animal Rescue

Shelter, fodder and medical care for rescued, abandoned and injured cows.

📚

Gurukul

Value-based education for children, combining studies, Sanskrit, and life skills.

🌳

Environment

Tree plantation, organic farming and awareness drives for a greener Uttarakhand.

🚩

Prabhat Feri

Morning community rallies spreading awareness on cow protection and cleanliness.

🪔

Yagya & Satsang

Regular spiritual gatherings that bring the community together.

🤝

Community Support

Working with local villagers, volunteers and partners to multiply impact.
